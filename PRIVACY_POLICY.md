# Privacy Policy for GradeTrack Pro

**Last updated:** [FILL IN DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]

## Overview

GradeTrack Pro ("the app") is an SGPA/CGPA calculator for Pakistani university
students. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Data Collection

**GradeTrack Pro does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

The app requires no account, no login, and no signup. All data you enter —
course names, marks, grades, credit hours, semester records, CGPA history,
and your optional display name — is stored **only on your own device**,
using local storage (AsyncStorage). This data:

- Is never sent to any server, because the app has no backend server.
- Is never shared with the developer, Anthropic, or any third party.
- Is never used for advertising, analytics, or tracking.
- Remains fully under your control at all times.

## Data You Can Export

The app includes an optional "Backup Data" feature (Settings screen) that
lets you export your saved data to a JSON file on your own device, which
you may then share or store however you choose (e.g., Google Drive, email
to yourself). This export is entirely user-initiated — the app never does
this automatically or without your action.

## Third-Party Services

GradeTrack Pro does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising,
or tracking SDKs. The app links out to each university's official website
(opened in your device's browser) only when you tap the "Visit Official
Website" button — that visit is subject to the respective university's own
privacy policy, not this one.

## Permissions

GradeTrack Pro requests no special Android permissions beyond what is
required to save files you explicitly choose to export or import via the
Backup/Restore feature.

## Children's Privacy

GradeTrack Pro is intended for university-level students and does not
knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time via Settings → Clear All Data.
Since no data is stored outside your device, this permanently and
completely removes it.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the
revised policy.

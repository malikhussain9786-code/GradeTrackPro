## Full description (max 4000 characters)

GradeTrack Pro is a premium, accurate SGPA and CGPA calculator built
specifically for Pakistani university students. No login, no signup, no
ads — just fast, reliable grade calculations using each university's
official grading system.

**Supports 10 official grading systems:**
Foundation University Islamabad, COMSATS University Islamabad, NUTECH,
NUML, International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI), Riphah
International University, FAST-NUCES, NUST, Air University, and Bahria
University — each using their real, verified official grade scales, not
generic assumptions.

**Features:**
• Calculate SGPA course-by-course, with live grade preview as you type
• Calculate CGPA by entering semester SGPAs, or import saved semesters
• Theory/Practical marks split support (matches COMSATS-style entry)
• GPA Planner — find out what SGPA you need next semester to hit your
  target CGPA
• Save and manage unlimited semesters, edit or delete anytime
• Full calculation history with search and filtering
• Academic dashboard with CGPA overview and SGPA trend chart
• Backup and restore your data locally — you always own your data
• Dark mode and light mode
• 100% offline — works without an internet connection
• No account required, no personal data collected

Whether you're tracking your first semester or planning your final CGPA
before graduation, GradeTrack Pro gives you accurate numbers you can
trust, calculated exactly the way your university does it.
